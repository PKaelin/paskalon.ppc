# Design
Designs that span multiple solutions, such as cross-service workflows, system-wide failover and end-to-end control flow.
Designs owned by one solution live in that solution's `Docs/Design` folder.


## Designs
- [Config - Domain - Application](./ConfigDomainApplication.md): how the microservices are built from their configuration at startup.
- [Power Sign Convention](./PowerSignConvention.md): sign of power values (export positive, import negative).
- [Telemetry](./Telemetry.md): metrics, logs and traces, including the metrics publisher.
- [Fault Tolerance & Resilience](./FaultTolerance.md): dependency health and behavior during outages.
- [Failover](./Failover.md): primary/secondary failover and high availability.
- [Colors](./Colors.md): colors and shapes shared by the user interfaces.
