Your power plant controller does exactly what the grid asks.
But can you prove it before you energize a single device?
And once it runs live on site, can you see how the real plant reacts compared with how a near-ideal plant should?

That's why I built paskalON Power Plant Control as two products that work as a pair: one runs your real plant, the other simulates a near-ideal one, so you can compare how the plant reacts with how it should react.

⚡ PPC Controller runs your plant
→ Multiple dashboards, each showing a different view of your plant's status and activity
→ Operating modes that show what you requested, what was commanded, and when a limit took over
→ Event handling from info to critical, with a full acknowledge history: who, what, when
→ Independent microservices: if one drops out, the plant holds its last setpoint and resumes automatically

🧪 PPC Simulator is your near-ideal reference plant
→ Every inverter, battery bank and meter speaks the real protocol: Modbus and IEEE C37.118
→ Inject faults, cut communication, add ramp limits and response delays, then watch the controller react
→ Find any of 100+ devices in seconds

Control it live. Prove it virtually.
